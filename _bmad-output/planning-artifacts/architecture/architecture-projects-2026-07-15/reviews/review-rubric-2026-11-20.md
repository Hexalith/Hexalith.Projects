# Architecture rubric review · November 20, 2026

**Verdict.** The spine covers all 25 FRs and 11 NFRs and identifies the read, writer and release boundaries. Two authorization decisions need tighter wording before implementing the affected writers. Three read and retention rules also admit incompatible implementations. This review applies to spine SHA-256 `9a725a27ced1446a65664fceb02bb5454ffbe29eb6f128710514dbe060e3c1c5` and Projects source `d6583c7f`.

| Priority | Finding and consequence | Proposed correction |

| High | **Folder replacement can outlive the evidence shown in Preview.** AD-5 and AD-13 bind versions generically. G-2 requires a versioned Folders reader-set digest. The spine never connects both Folders’ reader-set evidence to the confirmation check. An ACL-only change could leave the two resource versions intact while altering the people affected by the decision. The matrix also omits the Project User’s count-based disclosure. The November changes correctly separate verified native membership from advisory matches, conditional confidential UI from public McpCli, and 6.7 source evidence from broader release acceptance.
