## Supporting evidence for Finding 1 (pre-cutover v1 behavior)

- `src/Hexalith.Projects.Server/Conversations/ConversationsProjectConversationAssignmentDirectory.cs` is described as a "Projects write ACL adapter over the Conversations reassignment command client". It dispatches `ReassignConversationProjectCommand` for Assign and Clear.
- `src/Hexalith.Projects.Server/ProjectsDomainServiceEndpoints.cs`:
  - Lines 931–1020 call `assignmentDirectory.LinkAsync` and `MoveAsync`, and move requires `body.Confirmed == true`.
  - Lines 1143–1153 use `ReplacementConfirmed`.
  - Lines 628–630 set the ProjectId from the request body or from `Guid.NewGuid()`.
  - Lines 1890–1898 return 202 with caller-echoed task and correlation headers and no Location.
- `src/Hexalith.Projects.Server/Queries/ProposeNewProjectEndpoint.cs` lines 305–323 call `InMemoryProjectProposalConfirmationIdempotencyLedger.TryRecord(idempotencyKey, rootFingerprint)`. The key is not scoped by Tenant or actor, and the fingerprint covers only the body. A different Tenant reusing a key with a different body receives 409. AD-19 defines that 409 only for the same scoped key, and the Story 7.12 AC allows independent Tenants and actors to reuse keys. The memlog's exemption ("not an AD-5 implementation") does not appear in the spine.
