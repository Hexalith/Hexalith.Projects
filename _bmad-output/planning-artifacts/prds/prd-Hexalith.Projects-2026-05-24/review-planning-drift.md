Exercise an unbound Conversation after an explicit open, and a stale advisory link, as separate negative cases.
- **High — Terminal release paperwork still offers machine confirmation.** The canonical containment rule and terminal disposition make human MCP confirmation, Selection Evidence or even autonomous mutation conditional on a passed release gate (implementation-readiness-traceability-matrix.yaml lines 61 and 1038). Story 8.11 repeats the conditional grant (epics.md line 2855). PRD lines 36 and 91 and the November 15 scheduling decision lines 220 and 273 bar these actions on McpCli. A release owner could record a wider authorization than the product allows. *Fix:* Make the terminal disposition authorize the production release only and preserve Web/Chatbot-only confirmation. Extend the existing DW-84 wording reconciliation to the terminal matrix and dependent writer/selection criteria in epics.md lines 2032, 2046 and 2138.
- **High — The canonical smoke row can be satisfied with evidence from the wrong revision.** Its acceptance language requires a passing authenticated run and a test result file, without the candidate SHA, release profile, run/job identity, persisted state and recovery required by PRD line 98, epics.md lines 2732 and 2838, and sprint-status.yaml line 643 (implementation-readiness-traceability-matrix.yaml lines 994–1008). Keep the historical 19/56 result and the separate Contracts profiles distinct.

Readers could apply the supported route’s rules to retained traffic. *Fix:* Label the supported and retained contracts in the summary and attach Story 6.7’s D5 evidence to the FR-2/5/22 rows, including missing opt-in, denied permission and audit-write failure.
